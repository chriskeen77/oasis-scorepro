# 2026-07-06 — Founding the Keep

*Source: Claude Code remote session (oasis-scorepro)*

- Chris described the itch: chats and projects are silos; every conversation
  starts as a blank slate. He wants Claude to know him, his projects, and
  what's in flight — "one cohesive system."
- His original sketch: after every chat/code session, write a reflection to a
  personal memory — high points, what we're working on, thoughts, facts.
- Rejected the name "Christopher's Brain." Chose **the Keep** — from his own
  phrase "save the ones I want to keep," plus the castle tower meaning.
- Agreed design: plain markdown in `~/keep` (index, profile, projects,
  journal, archive), `/keep` and `/recall` commands in Claude Code, paste-in
  flow for claude.ai chats, and a weekly tending dashboard where Chris edits,
  promotes, and archives.
- Chris committed to stewardship: "I'll try to be a good steward toward it."
  Weekly dashboard review is his ritual, by his own choice.
- Built the whole v1 in this session; next step is installing it on his
  machine and living with it for a week.
